Fleet Start

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Fleet Start is a sailing race start timer. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not collect.

What we do not collect

Fleet Start does not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, phone number, or contacts, and has no access to your photo library. There are no advertising identifiers, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no third-party ad networks. You never create an account.

The one thing you can choose to give us is a sail number, described under Starting list below. It is optional, and it is the only place the app stores something you have typed about yourself.

The app can ask for your location, but only for the start-line tool described below, and your position never leaves your phone.

Anonymous sign-in

To keep races in sync, the app signs you in anonymously using Firebase Authentication. This creates a random identifier for your device. It is not linked to your Apple ID, Google account, email address, or any personal information, and it is used only to identify which device created a race so that only the race officer can control the sequence.

Race data

When a race officer creates a race, the following is stored in Firebase Realtime Database (Google Cloud, europe-west1):

  • the race name and optional class or fleet name entered by the officer
  • the chosen start sequence and class flag
  • the scheduled start time and the current race status
  • if the officer chooses a picture as the class flag, that picture

This information is visible to anyone using the app, because the list of active races is public by design so sailors can join easily. Do not enter personal information in the race or class name, and do not choose a personal photograph as a class flag.

Practice mode runs entirely on your device and stores nothing.

Class flag pictures

A race officer can supply a picture to use as the class flag — normally a class insignia, because we do not ship artwork for classes whose emblems we have no licence to redistribute.

The app does not read your photo library. It uses the system photo picker, which runs outside the app and hands back only the single file you choose, so Fleet Start never has access to any other photo. That one picture is scaled down, stored with the race, and shown to everyone who joins it — treat it as public, and pick an insignia rather than a personal photograph.

The picture is re-encoded before it is stored, which discards any metadata the original carried, including the location where a photo was taken. Only the image itself is kept.

Starting list

A sailor can put their boat on a race's starting list so the race officer can see who is out. This is optional: the countdown, signals, flags and sounds all work whether or not you sign on, and nothing is entered for you.

If you do sign on, the app stores your sail number against the race, together with the anonymous identifier for your device and the time you signed on. Nothing else — the app does not ask for your name and there is nowhere to put one.

A sail number is painted on the boat and is public on the water, but in a small fleet it identifies a boat and often a person, so please treat it as identifying you:

  • Everyone who has joined that race can see the list.
  • Enter the sail number only. Do not put a name in the field.
  • Remove me on the starting-list screen deletes it immediately, and the race officer can remove an entry too.
  • It is deleted with the race it belongs to.

Your device also remembers the last sail number you entered, so you do not have to retype it each week. That copy stays on your phone and is removed when you tap Remove me or delete the app.

Finishing order

After a race the officer can record the order boats crossed the line, together with the usual abbreviations for a boat that did not finish. This is stored with the race and visible to everyone who has joined it, the same as the starting list.

  • The officer can add a boat by hand, so a sail number may be recorded for a boat that never signed on. Race results are normally posted on a club noticeboard, so this is ordinary for the sport, but it does mean an entry can appear that the boat did not enter itself. Ask the race officer to correct or remove it.
  • Only the race officer can write or change a finishing order. It is deleted with the race.

The app records the order and nothing else. It does not calculate points, series standings, or handicap-corrected times.

Location

If the race officer switches on the start-line tool for a race, the app asks for permission to use your location while you are using it. Your position is used on your own phone to work out where the start line is and how far you are from it. It is never sent to our servers, never stored, and never shared: it is not written to the race database or anywhere else off your device.

The tool is off unless the race officer turns it on for that race, and you can decline the location permission — the countdown, signals, flags and sounds all work without it.

Data retention

Race data, including any starting-list entries, is transient and relates only to a race in progress. Races older than 48 hours are hidden from the app. You can delete your own starting-list entry at any time from the starting-list screen, and deleting it removes it immediately rather than hiding it.

Data processors

Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) processes this data on our behalf. See Google's privacy policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Signing on to a starting list is the only thing that stores something you have typed. You can remove it yourself at any time with Remove me, and the race officer can remove entries from their own race.

If you have not signed on to a starting list, nothing is stored that is tied to you, so there is nothing to request, export, or delete. Deleting the app removes the anonymous identifier and the remembered sail number from your device.

If you want an entry removed and cannot do it yourself, write to the address below with the race code and the sail number.
